1. Team Report
Status Update for TA
This week focused on "Quality of Life" (QoL) improvements and high-level feature additions to push Textplorer toward a gold standard. We transitioned from a functional game to a polished experience by streamlining navigation, fixing hitbox inconsistencies, and adding a "Ghost Runner" system to encourage competitive replayability. This marks our final formal status report before the project's conclusion.

1.2 Progress and Issues
Progress:

UI Transparency & Aesthetics: Improved visual clarity and modern feel by implementing semi-transparent backgrounds for HUD elements and menus.

Restart Mechanic: Implemented the R key as a global hotkey for instant level restarts, significantly reducing friction during difficult platforming sections.

Hitbox Refinement: Optimized collision masks for small and thin character models to ensure "pixel-perfect" precision and fairness.

Ghost Runner System: Added a "Ghost" sprite—a semi-transparent player model—that replays the path of the current leaderboard record-holder, giving players a visual target to chase.

Layout Fixes: Resolved a critical UI bug where the footer was overlapping and blocking interaction with bottom-tier menu buttons.

What Worked: The addition of the R key for restarts was a massive win for playability; it changed the player's psychology from frustration to "one more try."

What We Learned: Implementing the Ghost Runner taught us a lot about data serialization. Storing coordinate arrays for the ghost required more optimization than we initially anticipated to prevent frame drops on longer levels.

Trouble/Stuck: Integrating the sound engine is taking slightly longer than expected due to some library conflicts with the parser's automated scripts, but as we are in the final phase, this is being treated as a "stretch goal."
