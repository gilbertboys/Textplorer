# Weekly Status Report: Textplorer
**Date:** March 1st, 2026  
**Project Phase:** Systems Integration & Polishing

---

## 1. Team Report

**Status Update for TA:** This week marked a major transition from a "working prototype" to a "playable game." We have successfully implemented the core gameplay loop: players can now select a level, play with refined physics, track their speed via a new timer system, and post their scores to a functional leaderboard.

### 1.1 Goals from Last Week
* **Refine Physics & Collision:** Refine collisions to improve player experience. *(Completed)*
* **Automated Test Completion:** Finish writing the unit tests for the parser. *(In Progress)*
* **UI/UX Polish:** Add a "Back to Menu" button and "Error Log." *(Completed)*
* **Beta Level Set:** Design five "Official" levels of increasing difficulty. *(Completed)*

### 1.2 Progress and Issues
* **Progress:**
    * **Variable Jump Physics:** Improved the "feel" of movement by implementing variable jump height; the player now jumps higher the longer the **W** key is held.
    * **Time Tracking & Leaderboards:** A live timer now runs during gameplay. Upon reaching the goal, users are prompted to enter their name, which is then submitted along with their time to a level-specific leaderboard.
    * **Navigation & UX:** Significantly improved menu navigation. Users can now use the **Esc** key to exit menus, and "Back" buttons have been integrated into all UI screens.
    * **Sample Level Suite:** Finalized a set of 5 "Official" levels that showcase different mechanics and difficulty curves, providing a baseline for new players.

* **What Worked:** The implementation of the **Esc** key as a global "back" command greatly improved the game's professional feel and ease of navigation.
* **What We Learned:** Tuning variable jump height is a delicate balance. It required several iterations to ensure the player didn't feel "floaty" while still allowing for precision platforming.
* **Trouble/Stuck:** We are currently investigating a minor bug where the timer occasionally fails to stop if the player hits the goal and a hazard simultaneously.

### 1.3 Goals for Next Week
* **Bug Squashing:** Resolve the "Timer/Hazard" conflict and finalize the error log for invalid .txt files. *Estimate: 2 days*
* **Enhanced Feedback:** Add visual cues (particles or animations) when a player successfully submits a score. *Estimate: 2 days*
* **Sound Integration:** Begin adding basic sound effects for jumping, dying, and level completion. *Estimate: 3 days*
* **Testing Report:** Finalize the formal testing document based on the automated parser tests. *Estimate: 2 days*
