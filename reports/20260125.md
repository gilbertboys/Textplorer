**Date:** January 27, 2026

## 1. Team Report

**Status Update for TA:** The team has officially moved from a high-level concept to the initial research phase. Our primary focus this week was "environmental discovery"—transitioning our mindset from the Command Line Interfaces (CLI) we are used to, toward a web-based graphical environment. We spent time analyzing existing JavaScript-based platformers to understand how they handle movement and physics. We have finalized our decision to use the **Phaser** framework to ensure the game is lightweight and accessible via a web browser.

### 1.1 Goals from Last Week
* **N/A:** (Initial Project Kickoff / Outline phase).

### 1.2 Progress and Issues
* **Progress:** * Completed the **Project Outline**, defining our core goals and technical approach.
    * Finalized team roles and established our communication "hub" on Discord.
    * Selected our tech stack: **JavaScript/Phaser** for the engine and **GitHub Pages** for hosting.
* **What Worked:** * Our collaborative research into other JS web games helped us visualize how our "text-to-level" concept will actually look on screen.
* **What we Learned:** * We identified a "syntax shock" risk: moving from standard linear C++ logic to the asynchronous, loop-based nature of JavaScript and Phaser will be our first major learning curve.
* **Trouble/Stuck:** * We are currently just beginning to look at how to handle File I/O in a browser, as browsers have stricter security rules for reading local `.txt` files than the C++ environments we've used previously.

* **The "Hello World" Window:** Get a blank Phaser canvas to render in a web browser. **Estimate: 2 days**
* **Initial Legend Drafting:** Start a list of which keyboard symbols (like `#`, `^`, `@`) will map to which game objects. **Estimate: 1 day**
