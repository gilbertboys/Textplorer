About Textplorer

Textplorer is an online platforming game that turns .txt files into a playable environment!

Upload a .txt file from your computer to be able to jump around inside it.

GAME RULES:

  • Use WASD to move
  • All characters have collision that roughly follows what they look like

SPECIAL CHARACTERS:

  • * Is player spawn
  • $ Is the goal, the area you are trying to reach
  • ^ Is a spike, if you touch it you will restart

Press Esc to return to main menu
